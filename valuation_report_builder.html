Definition
✓ Forced Sale ✎ rename▼
A Forced Sale is one in which the assets are sold within a limited period of time, to one or more unconnected third parties with no special interest and does not take into consideration any element of Goodwill or Work in Progress. The physical assets are sold as a whole or individually and although sold without the benefit of any warranty or guarantee, are assumed to be in fair, working condition at the time of sale, unless otherwise stated. Such assets are sold wherever possible in situ.
✓ Open Market ✎ rename▼
An Open Market valuation is what we would expect to achieve from a well-advertised sale in situ. This valuation assumes the following: a willing seller, that all assets are complete, well maintained and in full working order, that there will be sufficient time to prepare sales details, advertise and conduct a sale, there is easy access for removal and that all items are wholly owned by the company.
